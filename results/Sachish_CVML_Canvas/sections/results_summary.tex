\subsection{VICReg vs.\ SigReg: Agg Head Bypassed}
\label{sec:results-summary-vicreg-sigreg}

(\texttt{cls\_agg\_head=false}, $z$ used as-is) vs.\ \texttt{..\_agghead}: a trivial
\texttt{num\_patches=1} aggregation head added, read \emph{encoder-only}
(\texttt{model.encoder(x)}, bypassing the head --- see
Section~\ref{sec:methodology-architecture}). SSv2 validation, same metric suite as

\paragraph{Inference} Once the agg head is bypassed, VICReg and SigReg are
indistinguishable to within noise on every metric --- the regularizer choice has no
detectable effect here, consistent with this log's broader finding that the agg head
itself, not the choice of regularizer, is what drives collapse.
\texttt{effective\_rank\_fraction} is the only metric that's green across the board;
\texttt{distance\_to\_goal\_spearman}, \texttt{trajectory\_straightness}, and
\texttt{direction\_reversal\_fraction} are red for all four, meaning rank-recovery via
bypassing the agg head is not sufficient to beat frozen DINOv2 CLS on temporal/geometric
quality --- recovering from collapse brings these checkpoints back to the no-agg-head
baseline's level, not past it.

\paragraph{Inference} Unlike the VICReg/SigReg pair, the no-regularizer checkpoints are
the only ones in the cls family to land green on \texttt{trajectory\_straightness} and
\texttt{direction\_reversal\_fraction} --- both VICReg and SigReg actually make
straightness \emph{worse} than doing nothing (Section~\ref{sec:results-summary-vicreg-sigreg}).
\texttt{distance\_to\_goal\_spearman} is also green here, where it was red for every
VICReg/SigReg column. The trade-off: \texttt{effective\_rank\_fraction} and
\texttt{time\_progress\_spearman} are red for both noreg columns, and
\texttt{content+change.linear} is red for \texttt{window0} specifically (the only cell
in either table where the no-agg-head reading is red while its agg-head-bypassed
counterpart is not). Consistent with the training-curve finding elsewhere in this log:
these checkpoints stop improving almost immediately (best epoch 7--8 of 50), so this is
an early, undertrained snapshot rather than a fully converged one.
